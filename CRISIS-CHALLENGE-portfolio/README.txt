CRISIS CHALLENGE — portfolio pictures and text

LINK: https://cntrl-alt-togetherroomchallenge.netlify.app

WHAT IT IS
Crisis Challenge is a live group game for a room. Everyone takes on the same identity, such as
Bakers, DJs or Trotro drivers. A crisis appears on screen, such as a postponed election or a
leader who will not step down. Everyone has 25 seconds to think, then anyone can share what
they would do. It runs over ten rounds on one shared screen and was the closing game of the
Together Room.

WHO IT'S FOR
Groups of people in a room together, such as participants at civic and cultural events.
No expertise is needed. The identities are everyday jobs, so everyone has a way in.

WHY IT'S IMPORTANT
Real civic problems do not arrive with a briefing. The game puts a room inside ten of them and
asks what each person would do. The crises cover three themes from the African Charter on
Democracy, Elections and Governance: representation of marginalised social groups,
constitutional changes of government, and democratic culture. After each answer the room
reflects on whether the identity they were given shaped their thinking.

WHEN WE USED IT
Used at the Creative Democracy Lab by AU/ECOSOCC and WCE Studios.
